🚌 TransitPulse

Predict hourly bus ridership for one MTA bus route in New York, to help planners decide where buses are needed.

Predicted riders for every hour of that day; the chosen hour is highlighted.

Limits: the model only knows the 24 routes it was trained on, and it learned from January to September 2025. Later dates use Open-Meteo weather (forecasts reach 16 days ahead) and are rough guesses, especially for March and October to December, which the model never saw. Typical error on the test period was about 65 riders per route-hour (about 14.5% of an average hour); the ± range uses the validation error (56 riders). Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0).

Gradient Boosting model trained on MTA bus ridership, MTA schedules and Open-Meteo weather. The same model runs here in your browser: its 500 trees were exported from scikit-learn, and the predictions were checked to match the Python model.
